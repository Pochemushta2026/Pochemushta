Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pochemushta VideoFactory handles information when an account owner connects the application to supported social media platforms.

2. Information we process

The application may process account authorization information provided by a connected platform, basic account/profile information made available through authorized APIs, video files selected for publication, publication metadata such as captions and titles, and identifiers or status information returned after publication.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the account owner, prepare requested content, upload or publish content, obtain publication status, and maintain the application's publishing workflow.

4. Sharing

Information is not sold. Data is shared with a social media platform only as necessary to perform an action requested and authorized by the account owner, or when required by law.

5. Storage and security

Authorization credentials and workflow data may be stored locally on the operator's device. Reasonable measures are used to protect stored credentials and application data.

6. Third-party services

Use of connected social media platforms is also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

7. Data deletion and access revocation

The account owner may revoke the application's access through the connected platform's account settings. Locally stored authorization credentials can also be deleted from the device running the application.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
