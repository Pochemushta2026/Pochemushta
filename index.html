Official website · Creator Studio

Short educational video workflow, from idea to publication.

Pochemushta Creator Studio is a desktop content workflow used by the Pochemushta team to prepare, review, and publish short educational videos to supported social media platforms.

Content workflow

Prepare short-form videos

Organize a video project, captions, publishing metadata, and final media before it is sent to a connected platform.

Owner-controlled

Review before publishing

The account owner remains in control of the content and decides what is submitted or published through authorized integrations.

Official APIs

Connect supported platforms

Third-party accounts are connected only through the platform's official authorization flow and only after the account owner grants access.

What the service does

Video preparation

Pochemushta Creator Studio supports the internal production workflow for short educational videos, including project organization, media selection, captions, and publication metadata.

Publishing workflow

When a platform integration is enabled, the application can submit a selected video and its publication details using the permissions authorized by the connected account owner.

Publication status

The application may receive identifiers and status information returned by the connected platform so the operator can confirm whether a requested publication was completed.

Local operator workflow

The application is operated as a desktop tool. Authorization credentials and workflow data may be stored locally on the operator's device.

How platform publishing works

Authorize

The account owner connects a supported platform using its official authorization mechanism.

Prepare & review

The operator selects the final video, caption, and available publication options inside Creator Studio.

Publish

The application sends the requested content through the authorized API and receives the resulting publication status.

Account access & data handling

Authorization

Pochemushta does not ask users to send social media passwords to the application. Supported accounts are connected through the platform's own authorization flow.

Data use

Authorized account information, selected video files, publication metadata, and returned status identifiers are processed only as needed to perform the requested workflow.
